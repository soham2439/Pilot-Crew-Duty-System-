# Flight Duty Regulations & RAG Knowledge Base

## 1. Flight Time Limits
- Daily maximum flight time limit: 8 hours.
- Weekly maximum flight time limit: 40 hours.
- Monthly maximum flight time limit: 100 hours.
- Annual maximum flight time limit: 1000 hours.

## 2. Rest Requirements
- Minimum rest period before any flight duty: 10 consecutive hours.
- The rest period must be increased to 12 hours if the preceding duty exceeded 10 hours.
- After a long-haul flight (exceeding 8 hours of flight time), a minimum rest period of 14 hours is required.
- Weekly rest: Pilots must have at least one continuous rest period of 36 hours (including two local nights) within any 7-day period.

## 3. Duty Types and Codes
- **FDUT**: Flight Duty (active flying duty).
- **DOFF**: Day Off (scheduled rest day, free from all operational duties).
- **VAC**: Vacation / Annual Leave.
- **SICK**: Sick Leave (unfit for duty).
- **AVBL**: Available / Standby (on-call for operational assignments).

## 4. Conflict Resolution & Standby
- Standby duty (AVBL) must not exceed 12 consecutive hours.
- If called for duty during standby (AVBL), the total combined standby and flight duty period cannot exceed 16 hours.
- Scheduling conflicts occur when two duties overlap in time or when rest periods between duties are less than the required minimums.
